# KPI Dictionary

## Revenue

Definition: Revenue is the total sales amount generated by transactions.

Formula: `Revenue = Quantity x Unit Price`

Interpretation: A higher revenue means the business sold more volume, higher-priced products, or both.

## Cost

Definition: Cost is the total internal cost linked to sold quantities.

Formula: `Cost = Quantity x Unit Cost`

Interpretation: Cost shows how much the company spent to deliver the sold products.

Use case: Cost is used to evaluate profitability and compare the internal expense of products, categories, countries, or customer segments.

## Margin

Definition: Margin is the gross profit generated by sales.

Formula: `Margin = Revenue - Cost`

Interpretation: A higher margin means the business keeps more value after covering product cost.

Also known as: Gross Margin.

Use case: Margin is useful to rank countries, customers, products, categories, or segments by profitability.

## Gross Margin

Definition: Gross Margin is another name for Margin in this demo model.

Formula: `Gross Margin = Revenue - Cost`

Interpretation: Gross Margin shows how much value remains after product cost is removed from revenue.

## Margin %

Definition: Margin % is the share of revenue that remains as margin.

Formula: `Margin % = Margin / Revenue x 100`

Interpretation: Margin % helps compare profitability across countries, products, or customer segments even when revenue levels are different.

## Quantity

Definition: Quantity is the number of units sold in a transaction or across a group of transactions.

Interpretation: Quantity is useful to measure sales volume independently from price.

## Average Selling Price

Definition: Average Selling Price is the average price paid per unit sold.

Formula: `Average Selling Price = Revenue / Quantity`

Interpretation: This KPI helps identify pricing level, discount effect, or mix effect across products and customers.

Also known as: ASP.

Use case: ASP is useful when the business wants to understand whether revenue is driven by higher prices or by higher quantities.
